Do conspiracy scales all measure the same thing?

Four short scales of “conspiracy mentality” are often treated as interchangeable. A bifactor model separates what each item shares with all four from what is particular to its own scale. Hover over or tap an item to read it.

How to read it

In a bifactor model each item loads on one general factor and on one factor specific to its scale, and the two are uncorrelated. Squaring each standardized loading gives the share of the item’s variance that factor accounts for. A scale is a clean measure of the common trait when its items are mostly green. The number on each scale card, omega-hierarchical for the specific factor, is the share of the scale score’s variance that comes from the scale’s own factor rather than the common one.
